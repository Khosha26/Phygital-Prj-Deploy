About

EMBASSYGROUP

Embassy Group is one of India’s most established and diversified real estate institutions. With over 100 million square feet of commercial, residential, hospitality, services, and education spaces delivered and managed, we emphasise high standards of quality, world-class services, environmental management, and safety. Founded in 1993, our operations now span 22+ cities, including Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, and Pune, amongst others.

Anchored in a vision of generating lasting impact through innovation, passion, and thoughtful execution, Embassy Group is driven by a commitment to deliver more – more quality and more value, while creating places and ecosystems that improve lives, strengthen communities, and transform everyday living.

Embassy Group’s portfolio includes three listed entities – Embassy Developments Limited, Embassy REIT, and WeWork India – reflecting the Group’s scale, transparency, and institutional approach to real estate. We strive to build a resilient ecosystem and proactively foster the social, economic, and environmental development of the communities we work in.
