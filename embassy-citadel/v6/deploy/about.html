— Our Journey
1993
— The Beginning
Embassy Group is founded in Bangalore by Jitu Virwani with a singular vision: to build spaces that transform skylines and enrich lives.
2006
— Expanding Horizons
Embassy Manyata opens in Bangalore — among India's largest integrated business parks. New asset classes, new cities, landmark developments.
2015
— Shaping Skylines
Embassy REIT is conceived — to become India's first publicly listed REIT in 2019. A new standard for institutional-grade real estate.
2024
— The Future
Embassy Citadel rises on Worli sea-face. A single tower. A world unto itself. Our finest expression of everything we believe in.
